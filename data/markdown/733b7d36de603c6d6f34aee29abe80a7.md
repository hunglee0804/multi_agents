# SCOPE AND OBJECTIVES
# COMMITMENTS
FPT Software is committed to developing and deploying AI in a responsible, safe, and sustainable manner, aligned
with our organizational purpose and delivering real value to communities and businesses.
This policy provides a foundation for setting AI objectives, ensuring compliance with applicable requirements, and
committing to the continual improvement of our AI Management System (AIMS).
FPT Software shall ensure:
## 1 Accountability & Ethics: Define clear responsibilities across the AI lifecycle; ensure ethical, lawful, and transparent
AI use, including informed consent, honesty in usage, and respect for company values.
## 2 AI Competence: Build expert teams and foster continuous learning in AI design, deployment, and management.
## 3 Data & Model Quality: Ensure data relevance, accuracy, and regular evaluation of AI model performance.
## 4 Fairness & Impartiality: Promote unbiased AI outcomes and conduct regular bias assessments.
## 5 Security & Privacy: Safeguard personal data and protect AI systems from unauthorized access and threats.
## 6 Safety & Sustainability: Design AI systems to be stable, maintainable, and non-harmful to people or the
environment.
## 7 Transparency & Explainability: Provide clear, understandable insights into AI decisions and maintain transparency
in development and deployment. Transparency in AI operations shall be ensured,  with clear information provided
on how AI systems function, the data they utilize, and their decision-making processes.
## 8 Human Oversight: Ensure human involvement throughout the AI lifecycle to maintain control, enable timely
intervention, and prevent unintended harm.
## 9 Compliance: Ensure all AI systems adhere to applicable laws, regulations, and standards to minimize legal risks
and support responsible AI management.
## 10 Risk Management: Establish a comprehensive framework to proactively identify, assess, and mitigate AI-related
risks, ensuring system safety and minimizing impact on users and the environment.
## 11 System Quality & Maintenance: Ensure AI systems meet quality and performance standards through clear
objectives, testing, monitoring, and regular maintenance. Implement protocols for timely issue detection and
resolution to maintain value and reliability.
## 12 Third-party and Customer Relationships: Observe third parties align with responsible AI practices and consider
customer expectations in the development and use of AI systems.
## 13 Responsible Use: Use approved AI tools only for legitimate business purposes, following legal, contractual, and
internal policies. Users must verify outputs and protect data and customer confidentiality.
## 14 Prohibited Use: Do not use AI for unauthorized, unethical, or malicious purposes, including data disclosure,
impersonation, or unapproved tools. Misuse or abuse is strictly forbidden.

# ACTIONS
# REFERENCES
FPT Software shall:
• Maintain and improve AIMS in accordance with ISO/IEC 42001:2023.
• Integrate AIMS into organizational planning and operations.
• Ensure legal compliance and effective AI risk management.
• Align with related policies (e.g., information security, quality, safety).
• Periodically review this policy to ensure its relevance and effectiveness.
• Establish procedures for handling exceptions and deviations.
• Assess the impact of AI systems on individuals, individual groups, and society.
This policy is documented, communicated internally, and made available to relevant stakeholders as appropriate.
